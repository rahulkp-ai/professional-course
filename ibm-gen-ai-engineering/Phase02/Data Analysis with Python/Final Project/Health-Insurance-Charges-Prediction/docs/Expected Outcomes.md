## Expected Outcomes

At the completion of the project, the system is expected to:

- **Data Preparation & Quality**
- Successfully load and preprocess the health insurance dataset.
- Identify and handle missing or invalid data.

- **Exploratory Analysis & Insights**
- Provide meaningful exploratory analysis of insurance charges.
- Identify important factors associated with insurance costs.

- **Model Development & Evaluation**
- Develop Simple Linear Regression models.
- Develop a Multiple Linear Regression model.
- Develop and evaluate a Ridge Regression model.
- Compare the predictive performance of the developed models.
- Identify the most suitable regression approach for the dataset.

- **Practical Application**
- Generate estimated insurance charges for previously unseen individuals.
