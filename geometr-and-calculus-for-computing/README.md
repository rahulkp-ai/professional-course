## 🎯 Overview

This repository archives notes, mathematical proofs, and core computational applications from the **Geometry and Calculus for Computing** curriculum by the University of London.

The goal of this coursework is to establish a rigorous, first-principles understanding of geometric spaces and multivariable calculus—transforming abstract continuous mathematics into concrete algorithms used in modern machine learning, computer graphics, and optimization pipelines.

---

## 🧠 Key Core Curriculums

### 1. Vector Geometry & Coordinate Spaces

- **Spatial Systems:** Cartesian and polar coordinate mapping in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $\mathbb{R}^3$.
- **Vector Operations:** Analytical and geometric applications of dot products (projections, orthogonality) and cross products (area, normal vectors).
- **Transformations:** Linear mappings, rotations, scaling matrices, and geometric translation mechanics.

### 2. Differential Calculus (Single Variable)

- **Limits & Continuity:** Establishing foundations for instantaneous rates of change.
- **Algorithmic Differentiation:** Analytical computation using Product, Quotient, and Chain Rules.
- **Optimization:** Locating critical points, global/local extrema, and tracking curve behavior.

### 3. Multivariable & Vector Calculus

- **Partial Differentiation:** Computing directional changes across multi-dimensional fields.
- **The Gradient Vector ($\nabla f$):** Geometrically interpreting the path of steepest ascent.
- **Directional Derivatives:** Quantifying rates of change along arbitrary vector trajectories.

### 4. Computational Implementations

- Formulating loss surfaces and computing gradient vectors mathematically.
- Building foundational intuition for backpropagation mechanics in Deep Learning.
